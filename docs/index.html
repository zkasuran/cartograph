Exact where the graph can be exact, silent where it cannot. For context the HERB paper reports off-the-shelf GraphRAG at about 10 and the best agentic RAG at about 33 on its blended 0-100 metric, so answering the structural half exactly and abstaining honestly is a real step. Free-text content synthesis (the long-context half) is deliberately out of scope for a graph engine.

Why HydraDB

The ontology is the product: Employee, Customer, Product, Org and Artifact nodes with typed edges (AUTHORED, REVIEWED, PARTICIPATED, ON_TEAM, REPORTS_TO, ABOUT). A person query walks from a topic's artifacts to their authors and returns exact ids. Ten employees named "Charlie Brown" are distinct nodes disambiguated by role, org and product neighbourhood. Abstention falls out of the traversal returning nothing.
